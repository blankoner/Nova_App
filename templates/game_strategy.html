Mini-game 01 — Strategy

Conquer the map — every move says something about how you think

How to play

  • You (violet) face the AI (red). Neutral grey territories are up for grabs.
  • Attack phase: click one of your territories with 2+ armies, then click an adjacent enemy or neutral territory to attack it.
  • Combat is dice-based: each side rolls one die per army, higher total wins. A territory must always leave 1 army behind.
  • When you run out of moves (or want to stop), end your attack phase.
  • Reinforce phase: click your territories to place {{ "3" }} fresh armies, then continue.
  • The AI then takes its turn. Wipe out all enemy territories to win.
